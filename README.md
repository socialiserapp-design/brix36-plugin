# Brix36 video library for Claude

This plugin lets Claude work with the finished Brix36 videos already in your account. Claude can find a video, play it, rename it, sort your videos by artist, and give you a download link. It never makes a new video and never spends credits.

If you ask to open or edit one of your finished videos in Brix36, Claude gives you a plain "Open in Brix36" link to that video.

## Status

The restricted Claude endpoint `https://api.brix36.com/mcp/anthropic-library` is now live. It finds, organises, renames, plays and exports your finished videos.

## What you need

A Brix36 account. You sign in through Brix36's own sign-in page, and Claude never sees your password.
